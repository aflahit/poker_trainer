## Key Concepts Tested

1. **Kicker awareness** — A7o, A5o, KJo, QTo look strong but crumble vs. better kickers at showdown
2. **Position shifts opening ranges** — KJo is a fold from UTG but a raise from Cutoff/Button
3. **Three-bet discipline** — Only the top ~5 hands continue into a 3-bet pot
4. **Set-mining stack requirement** — Small pairs need deep stacks; fold 66 vs raise with medium stacks
5. **Suited doesn't save junk** — K4s, Q5s, J6s are folds from early position regardless of suit
6. **Big blind discount** — BB gets a significant price reduction; defend wider than you think
7. **Opponent-type adjustments** — Tight players raise a narrower range; loose/calling-station opponents justify more value raises
8. **Short-stack raise-or-fold** — Never limp short-stacked; raise or fold only
